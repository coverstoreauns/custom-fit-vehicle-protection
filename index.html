Why the pattern matters more than the material

A generic cover is a compromise from the first cut — a loose average shape sold as though it fits everything. It rarely does.

Coverstore.au starts every product from a single vehicle's real dimensions, so airbag seams, sensor housings and stitching lines land exactly where the factory intended — before the material choice even comes into it.
